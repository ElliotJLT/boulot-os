# Master Experience Bank

> This is your SUPERSET CV — every role, every achievement, every bullet you might ever
> want to use, all in one place. You never send this. When you apply for a specific job,
> the AI PULLS the relevant bullets from here and arranges them into a tailored CV.
>
> Two rules:
> 1. Put EVERYTHING in here, even things that feel minor. You can't select a bullet that doesn't exist.
> 2. The AI will only ever reword and reorder what's here. It will NEVER invent experience. So this
>    file is the source of truth — make it complete and accurate.
>
> Tag each bullet with #hashtags for the skill area (e.g. #ops #ai #sales #regulated). Tags let
> the AI filter for the bullets that match a given job. Delete these quote lines as you go.
