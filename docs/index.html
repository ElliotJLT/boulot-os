The problem

Every other tool plays the numbers game. The numbers stopped working.

They optimise for volume — and leave your career data sitting on someone else's servers.

auto-apply botsSpray, then throttled

Fire 200 generic applications a day. Response rates collapsed because of them, and platforms now throttle the accounts that use them.

ai recruiter agentsTheir network, not yours

Hand you a daily list of often-stale matches. Their incentive is to place you in their network — not to make you the strongest candidate for the job you want.

trackers + autofillA bolt-on AI layer

Organise the chaos, but the AI is one-shot, generic, keyword-stuffed output that modern applicant-tracking systems now penalise.

What it is

The slow, careful work a great application actually takes — done for you.

Boulot runs on your computer through Claude Code. Not a website you log into — a folder you own. You talk to it in plain English.

the core mechanic

Your CV gets argued over, not generated.

Three agents with opposing jobs pressure-test every claim and disagree on purpose. You get the version that survived the fight — not a first draft from a single prompt.

Letters that say something only true about that company. Plus what's stale, what's next, what worked.

Where Boulot honestly doesn't compete: it won't find jobs for you, and it can't introduce you to a hiring manager the way a recruiter network can. If you want warm intros, the recruiter-agent tools do that well. Boulot does the other half — the half they do badly: making you impossible to pass over once you've found the role.
How to use it

You don't need to be technical. Set it up once, then just talk to it.

About twenty minutes to set up. After that you say "tailor my CV for this role" or "grill me for the interview" and it does the work.

1
Install Claude Code

A free app from Anthropic that lets the AI work with files on your computer. There's a desktop version — no terminal needed.

2
Download Boulot

One click from GitHub. It's a folder that lives on your machine.

3
Tell it who you are

Fill in two files — or just say "interview me and fill in my profile" and it writes them for you.

4
Start applying

Paste a job link. Ask for a tailored CV, a cover letter, research, a mock interview. It tracks the rest.
