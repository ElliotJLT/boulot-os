The problem

Every other tool plays the numbers game. The numbers stopped working.

The job-search tools everyone uses optimise for volume. That race already broke — and it leaves your career data sitting on someone else's servers.

Auto-apply bots

Fire 200 generic applications a day. Response rates collapsed because of them, and platforms now throttle the accounts that use them.

AI recruiter agents

Hand you a daily list of matches — often stale or off-target. Their incentive is to place you in their network, not to make you the strongest candidate for the job you want.

Trackers & autofill

Organise the chaos, but the AI layer is a bolt-on: one-shot, generic, keyword-stuffed output that modern applicant-tracking systems now penalise.

What it is

A system that does the slow, careful work a great application actually takes.

Boulot runs on your own computer through Claude Code. It's not a website you log into — it's a set of plain files and AI instructions in a folder you own. You talk to it in plain English.

  • Tailors your CV per rolePulls from a master record of everything you've done, then rewords and reorders it for that one job. Never invents experience.
  • Argues over your CV before you send itA Hiring Manager, a Reviewer, and a Strategist pressure-test every claim and disagree on purpose. You get the version that survived.
  • Researches the companyRecent news, funding, who you'd report to, red flags — before you apply or interview.
  • Drafts cover letters that aren't genericSays something only true about that company, framed through your experience.
  • Grills you for the interviewA friendly interviewer, a hostile one, and a gap-analyst run a tough mock and tell you where you're bluffing.
  • Tracks every applicationKnows what's stale, what's next, and what worked across everything you've sent.

How to use it

You don't need to be technical. Set it up once, then just talk to it.

About twenty minutes to set up. After that you say things like "tailor my CV for this role" or "grill me for the interview" and it does the work.

Install Claude Code

A free app from Anthropic that lets the AI work with files on your computer. There's a desktop version — no terminal needed.

Download Boulot

One click from GitHub. It's a folder that lives on your machine.

Tell it who you are

Fill in two files — who you are and everything you've done. Or just say "interview me and fill in my profile" and it writes them for you.

Start applying

Paste a job link. Ask for a tailored CV, a cover letter, company research, a mock interview. It tracks the rest.
