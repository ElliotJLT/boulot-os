How it works

It's not a magic button. It's a system that does the real work, with you.

Boulot lives on your computer and runs through Claude Code. No account, no cloud — just a folder that's yours. Here's the loop it runs:

1

It learns the real you

A living profile and a master CV that hold everything you've ever done — every role, every number — tagged so nothing gets lost. This is the source of truth it draws from, and it grows as you do. It never makes anything up.

2

It runs your pipeline

Roles move from "maybe" to "applying" to "done." It researches companies for you — news, funding, who you'd report to, red flags — and helps you find the ones actually worth your time.

3

It builds each application by hand

For every role it pulls the right bullets from your master CV, then three agents argue over the draft — the hiring manager, a sharp second reader, and a strategist hunting your hidden strengths — until it holds up. You get the version that survived.

4

It gets you ready

Cover letters that say something true about that company, then a proper mock interview: a friendly interviewer, a hostile one, and someone who finds exactly where you're bluffing — so the real thing feels easy.

5

It tracks everything and learns

What's stale, what's next, what's landing. Every application teaches it something, so the next one is sharper. The grind stops living in your head.

Getting started

You don't need to be techie. Set it up once, then just chat to it.

About twenty minutes to get going. After that you say things like "tailor my CV for this one" or "give me a tough mock interview" and it gets on with it.

1
Install Claude Code

A free app from Anthropic that lets the AI work with files on your computer. There's a desktop version — no terminal needed.

2
Download Boulot

One click from GitHub. It becomes a folder that lives on your machine.

3
Tell it about you

Fill in two short files — or just say "interview me and fill in my profile" and it writes them for you.

4
Start applying

Paste a job link. Ask for a tailored CV, a cover letter, some homework, a mock interview. It keeps track of the rest.
