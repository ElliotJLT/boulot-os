How it works

No magic button. A system that does the real work alongside you.

Boulot lives on your computer and runs through Claude Code. No account, no cloud, just a folder that's yours. Here's the loop it runs:

1

It learns the real you

The first time you run it, it interviews you and writes a living profile and a master CV that hold everything you've ever done, every role and every number. That's what each new CV gets built from, and it grows as you do. It never invents anything.

2

It runs your pipeline

Roles move from "maybe" to "applying" to "done." It researches each company for you: news, funding, who you'd report to, any red flags. And it helps you find the ones actually worth your time.

3

It builds each application by hand

For every role it pulls the right bullets from your master CV, then three agents argue over the draft: the hiring manager, a sharp second reader, and a strategist hunting your hidden strengths. They go at it until the draft holds up, and you get the version that survived.

4

It gets you ready

Cover letters that say something true about that company, then a proper mock interview: a friendly interviewer, a hostile one, and someone who finds exactly where you're bluffing. By the time the real one comes round, you've already had the hard version.

5

It tracks everything and learns

What's stale, what's next, what's landing. Every application teaches it something, so the next one is sharper. The grind stops living in your head.

Getting started

No terminal skills needed. Set it up once, then talk to it like a person.

About twenty minutes to set up. From then on you type in plain English and it does the work.

1
Install Claude Code

A free app from Anthropic that lets the AI work with files on your computer. There's a desktop version, so no terminal needed.

2
Download Boulot

One click from GitHub. It becomes a folder on your machine.

3
Type /start

Boulot interviews you one question at a time and writes your profile and CV from your answers. Paste an old CV or your LinkedIn to go faster. Nothing to fill in by hand.

4
Apply for your first role

Paste a job link and say "tailor my CV for this." You get a tailored CV, a cover letter, company research, and a mock interview. Boulot tracks the rest.

Talking to it sounds like this:

  • "Read my profile and tell me which roles I should go for."
  • "I want to apply for Head of Ops at Acme. Here's the link."
  • "Tailor my CV for it, then make a PDF."
  • "Give me a tough mock interview for this one."
