The honest problem

Most job tools just help you send more spam, faster.

They're built for volume. That race already broke — and your CV ends up sitting on someone else's servers.

📨

Auto-apply bots

Fire off 200 identical applications a day. Reply rates fell because of them, and the big platforms now throttle the accounts that use them.

🤝

AI recruiter agents

Hand you a daily list of often-stale matches. Their job is to place you somewhere in their network — not to make you the best candidate for the role you actually want.

🗂️

Trackers & autofill

Tidy, but the AI bit is bolted on: one-shot, generic, keyword-stuffed text that today's hiring systems quietly mark down.

What it does

It does the slow, careful work a good application really takes.

Boulot lives on your computer and runs through Claude Code. There's no account to log into — just a folder that's yours. You talk to it like a person.

✍️

It plays devil's advocate on your CV — before a recruiter does.

Three helpers look at your draft from different angles: the hiring manager, a sharp second reader, and someone hunting for your hidden strengths. They disagree, then settle it. You get the version that held up.

How it works

You don't need to be techie. Set it up once, then just chat to it.

About twenty minutes to get going. After that you say things like "tailor my CV for this one" or "give me a tough mock interview" and it gets on with it.

1
Install Claude Code

A free app from Anthropic that lets the AI work with files on your computer. There's a desktop version — no terminal needed.

2
Download Boulot

One click from GitHub. It becomes a folder that lives on your machine.

3
Tell it about you

Fill in two short files — or just say "interview me and fill in my profile" and it writes them for you.

4
Start applying

Paste a job link. Ask for a tailored CV, a cover letter, some homework, a mock interview. It keeps track of the rest.
