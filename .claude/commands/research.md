# /research

Deep research dive on a company for an active application.

1. Identify the company
2. Spawn agent team:
   - **Company Intel**: Web search for latest news, funding rounds, leadership changes, product launches. Focus on last 6 months.
   - **Market Analyst**: Competitive landscape, market size, regulatory environment, industry trends
   - **Culture Scout**: Glassdoor reviews, LinkedIn team analysis, company values, interview process reports
3. Compile findings into `USER/active/{company}/research.md`
4. Highlight anything that should influence interview prep or CV tailoring
5. Flag any red flags (layoffs, bad reviews, funding concerns)
