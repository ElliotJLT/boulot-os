# Question Bank — Questions to Ask Interviewers

## About the Role
- What does the first 90 days look like in this role?
- What's the biggest challenge the person in this role will face in the first 6 months?
- How is success measured for this position?
- What happened to the previous person in this role?
- What's the team structure and who would I work with most closely?

## About the Team & Culture
- How does the team handle disagreements about direction or priorities?
- What's the decision-making process like — centralised or distributed?
- How has the team changed in the last year?
- What's the balance between planned work and reactive/firefighting?

## About the Company
- What's the company's biggest strategic priority right now?
- Where do you see the company in 2-3 years?
- What's the biggest risk to the business right now?
- How does this department fit into the wider company strategy?

## About Growth
- What does career progression look like from this role?
- How does the company invest in professional development?
- Are there opportunities to take on scope beyond the core role?

## Red Flag Detectors
- "How would you describe the work-life balance?" (watch for hesitation)
- "What's the attrition rate been like recently?" (direct but fair)
- "What would a former employee say is the worst thing about working here?"

## Power Questions (Pick One to Close)
- "Is there anything about my background that gives you pause for this role?" (lets you address concerns directly)
- "Based on our conversation, what do you think my biggest challenge would be?"
- "What would make you say in 6 months that this hire was a great decision?"
