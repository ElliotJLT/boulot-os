# Salary Negotiation Framework

## Before the Conversation
- Research market rate for role, level, and location (Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, LinkedIn Salary)
- Know your walk-away number and your target number
- Prepare your value justification with specific achievements and metrics

## Key Principles
1. **Never give your number first** — let them anchor. If pressed: "I'd love to understand the full package before discussing numbers."
2. **Talk total comp, not base** — base + bonus + equity + benefits + flexibility
3. **Use ranges, not points** — "I'm looking at £120-140k base depending on the full package"
4. **Anchor high but defensibly** — your range floor should be your actual target
5. **Always negotiate** — even a small bump shows you value yourself

## Scripts

### When asked early: "What are your salary expectations?"
> "I'm focused on finding the right role and I'm flexible on comp. I'd love to understand the range you've budgeted for this position."

### When you have an offer:
> "Thank you, I'm excited about this. The base of £X is below my expectations given [specific value you bring]. Based on my research and experience with [specific achievement], I was expecting closer to £Y. Is there flexibility?"

### If they say no room on base:
> "I understand. Could we look at [signing bonus / equity / review timeline / additional leave / remote flexibility]?"

## What NOT to Do
- Don't apologise for negotiating
- Don't reveal your current salary (illegal to ask in many jurisdictions)
- Don't accept on the spot — "I'd like 24-48 hours to review the full package"
- Don't make threats or ultimatums
