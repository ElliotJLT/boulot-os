# STAR Method Framework

## Structure
- **Situation**: Set the scene. Where, when, what was the context? (10-15 seconds)
- **Task**: What was your specific responsibility or challenge? (10 seconds)
- **Action**: What did YOU do? Be specific — tools, decisions, steps. (30-40 seconds)
- **Result**: What happened? Quantify: numbers, percentages, outcomes. (15-20 seconds)

## Rules
- Total answer: 60-90 seconds max
- Always use "I" not "we" — interviewers want YOUR contribution
- Lead with the result if it's impressive, then explain how you got there
- Every answer needs at least one specific metric or outcome
- If you don't have exact numbers, use reasonable estimates and say so

## Common Mistakes
- Too much situation, not enough action
- Vague actions ("I worked with the team to...")
- No measurable result
- Story too long — edit ruthlessly
- Using "we" throughout — own your contribution
