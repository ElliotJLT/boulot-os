## Self-Test (Retrieval Practice)

> Close this document. Answer these from memory. Then check back.

1. What is [Name]'s #1 belief about [their domain]?
2. What story would you tell if they ask about [likely topic]?
3. What metric/number backs up that story?
4. What question will you ask them that shows you've done your homework?
5. What's the one thing you should NOT say to this person?

---

## Prep Protocol

> Based on cognitive science research (dual coding, spaced repetition, retrieval practice).

| When | What to do | Time |
|------|-----------|------|
| **3+ days before** | Read full research. Build this brief. | 30-45 min |
| **2 days before** | Close notes. Write down everything you remember about the interviewer. Compare with brief. Note gaps. | 15 min |
| **1 day before** | Quiz with a partner (one reads the belief/value, the other gives the matching story). Review if-then playbook. | 15 min |
| **Morning of** | Scan BLUF + beliefs table + if-then playbook only. Do NOT re-read full research. | 5 min |
| **10 min before** | One deep breath. Recall your opening line and your 2 questions. That's it. | 2 min |
